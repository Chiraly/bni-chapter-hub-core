# Topic bank — visitors, invitations, conversion, growth

For chapters with low `Vis`, low `Conv`, or flat `Growth`.

Each entry is a seed, not a script. Take the one thing and the hook, then write it
properly using `education-moment-method.md`. `CEU:` gives search terms for
`build_ceu_library.py --hour <terms>`.

---

### 1. Who you already know
**One thing.** You are not looking for strangers — you already know who to invite.
**Gaps.** Vis
**Business hook.** Every business has a list it never looks at: past clients, the supplier who does good work, the person who fixed your hot water. Nobody mines it.
**BNI link.** Ten names from your phone contacts, in ten minutes, is the entire invitation problem solved. Most members have never once sat down and made the list.
**Visitor beat.** Someone in this room thought of you specifically. That is not a mailout.
**Ask.** Open your contacts. Write down three names before you leave the car park.
**Presenter.** A member who brings visitors regularly.
**CEU.** invite visitors invitation

### 2. The invitation that doesn't feel like selling
**One thing.** Invite to the room, not to the membership.
**Gaps.** Vis
**Business hook.** Nobody minds being invited to something. Everyone minds being sold to. The difference is entirely in the sentence you use.
**BNI link.** "Come and see it, you don't have to do anything" is true, and it works because it is true. Members who feel awkward inviting are usually inviting people to *join*, which is a much bigger ask than turning up for breakfast.
**Visitor beat.** Whoever brought you did not sign you up for anything. You are here to look.
**Ask.** Use one sentence this week: "There's a group I go to on Tuesdays — come and have breakfast with me."
**Presenter.** A member who invites easily.
**CEU.** invite visitors invitation ask

### 3. What a visitor is worth
**One thing.** A visitor is worth something to the chapter even if they never join.
**Gaps.** Vis, Conv
**Business hook.** Every business knows its cost per lead. Most have no idea what one warm introduction is worth.
**BNI link.** A visitor brings their network into the room for an hour. They refer members afterwards. They tell people what they saw. Conversion is not the only return.
**Visitor beat.** You are not a prospect to us today. You are a guest, and guests get looked after.
**Ask.** Introduce your visitor to two members by name, not one.
**Presenter.** Visitor Host.
**CEU.** visitor value visitors

### 4. The first four minutes
**One thing.** A visitor decides how they feel about this chapter before anyone talks business.
**Gaps.** Conv
**Business hook.** Walk into a shop where nobody looks up. You have decided about that shop before a word is said.
**BNI link.** Open Networking is not warm-up — for a visitor it is the whole experience. Who greets them, whether anyone remembers their name, whether they stand alone holding a coffee.
**Visitor beat.** If you have been standing on your own this morning, that is our failure, not yours. Come and find me afterwards.
**Ask.** Next week, find the person you do not recognise before you find your friends.
**Presenter.** Visitor Host or a recent member.
**CEU.** visitor experience wow welcome

### 5. The seven days after
**One thing.** The follow-up decides it, not the meeting.
**Gaps.** Conv
**Business hook.** You fill in a contact form because you are genuinely interested. Nobody calls. A week later you have bought from someone else, and you do not even remember the first company's name.
**BNI link.** Visitors leave interested and go back to a busy week. If the only thing that reaches them is a generic email, interest fades on schedule. A phone call in the first 48 hours changes the number.
**Visitor beat.** Someone will ring you this week. Not to sell — to answer the questions you thought of on the drive home.
**Ask.** Ring one visitor from the last month. Today, not Friday.
**Presenter.** Membership Committee or Visitor Host.
**CEU.** follow up visitor conversion

### 6. Why they didn't join
**One thing.** Most visitors who don't join were never actually asked.
**Gaps.** Conv
**Business hook.** Sales training everywhere teaches the same lesson: the deal is lost far more often to silence than to objection.
**BNI link.** We are comfortable being welcoming and uncomfortable being direct. "Can you see yourself in the room?" is not pushy — it is the question the visitor is already asking themselves.
**Visitor beat.** If you have a question you think is rude, ask it. Cost, time, commitment — all fair.
**Ask.** If you invited someone recently, ask them straight out what they thought.
**Presenter.** Vice President.
**CEU.** visitor conversion ask membership

### 7. Inviting your competition
**One thing.** A full room refers more than a comfortable one.
**Gaps.** Vis, Growth
**Business hook.** Retail clusters. Cafés open next to cafés. Concentration creates traffic; it rarely divides it.
**BNI link.** Members protect their category, then wonder why the room is small. Adjacent trades, complementary categories, even people you think of as rivals — every empty seat is referrals nobody is passing.
**Visitor beat.** If your category is taken, say so. There are other chapters, and we would rather point you well than lose you.
**Ask.** Name one category the chapter is missing. Tell the Membership Committee today.
**Presenter.** A confident long-standing member.
**CEU.** invite competition chapter growth

### 8. The empty chairs
**One thing.** Chapter size is not vanity — it is referral capacity.
**Gaps.** Growth, Size
**Business hook.** A team of five covers less ground than a team of thirty. Nobody finds that surprising anywhere except here.
**BNI link.** More members means more networks, more categories, more referrals per person. Growth is not for the Leadership Team's report — it is for your invoice.
**Visitor beat.** Look around at the empty seats. One of them might suit someone you know.
**Ask.** Bring one name to the Membership Committee this week.
**Presenter.** President or Vice President.
**CEU.** chapter growth membership

### 9. Inviting online
**One thing.** Your existing audience is an invitation list you have already built.
**Gaps.** Vis
**Business hook.** Businesses spend years building a following and then never ask it for anything except a sale.
**BNI link.** One post, one story, one message to a group you are already in. Low effort, and it reaches people you would never think to ring.
**Visitor beat.** If you found us online, that is exactly how it is supposed to work.
**Ask.** Post once this week about Tuesday morning. One sentence and a photo.
**Presenter.** A member who is active online.
**CEU.** social media invite visitors

### 10. Bring one, every month
**One thing.** One visitor a month, from everyone, is a full chapter.
**Gaps.** Vis, Growth
**Business hook.** Consistency beats intensity in every part of business. Small and regular wins.
**BNI link.** The Power of One asks for one visitor a month. Not ten. Chapters fail this because three people bring six and everyone else brings none.
**Visitor beat.** Somebody kept a promise to themselves this month, and that is why you are here.
**Ask.** Put a monthly reminder in your phone right now, in the room, before you forget.
**Presenter.** Go For Green Coordinator or Education Coordinator.
**CEU.** power of one visitors invite

### 11. What we sound like from the doorway
**One thing.** The room's habits are invisible to us and very visible to a visitor.
**Gaps.** Conv
**Business hook.** Every workplace has in-jokes and acronyms that make total sense inside and none outside. New staff spend a month decoding them.
**BNI link.** PALMS, TYFCB, CEU, one-to-one, Power of One. We say these constantly. A visitor hears an in-group and quietly decides they are outside it.
**Visitor beat.** If we said a word this morning that meant nothing to you, you were right to be confused. Ask me and I will translate.
**Ask.** Say one acronym in full this week, for whoever is listening.
**Presenter.** A member who joined in the last year.
**CEU.** visitor experience welcome jargon

### 12. The substitute nobody thought of
**One thing.** Your substitute is a visitor with a reason to be interested.
**Gaps.** Vis, Abs
**Business hook.** Cover arrangements are usually treated as a chore. They are also an audition.
**BNI link.** If you cannot make it, sending someone keeps your seat working and gives that person a look at the room. Some of the best members arrived as somebody's substitute.
**Visitor beat.** If you are here filling in for someone today, you count as a guest and we are glad you came.
**Ask.** Pick your substitute now, before you need one.
**Presenter.** Membership Committee.
**CEU.** substitute attendance visitors

### 13. The visitor day
**One thing.** A concentrated push works better than a steady trickle, once or twice a year.
**Gaps.** Vis, Growth
**Business hook.** Retailers run sale days for a reason. A deadline creates action that an open invitation never does.
**BNI link.** Everyone invites for the same date. The room is full, the energy is different, and visitors see a chapter at its best rather than a half-empty one.
**Visitor beat.** If today feels busy, that is deliberate. This is us at full strength.
**Ask.** Diary the visitor day and name your two people today.
**Presenter.** President.
**CEU.** visitor day chapter growth invite
