# Topic bank — core values, Givers Gain, seasonal moments

Use these to open and close a term, to break up a run of corrective weeks, and to fit
the program to the Australian calendar. A chapter that only ever gets told what it is
doing wrong stops listening.

The seven BNI Core Values each have an official icon in `assets/icons-bni/`.

---

## Core values

### 1. Givers Gain, literally
**One thing.** Give first, without a scoreboard, and it comes back — but not from where you expect.
**Gaps.** Ref, Ret
**Business hook.** Everyone has been helped by someone who had nothing to gain, and remembered it for years.
**BNI link.** The philosophy only works if the giving is genuine. Give-to-get is transparent and the room can smell it.
**Visitor beat.** Nobody will ask you for anything today. That is not a technique, it is the actual rule.
**Ask.** Do one thing for a member this week that cannot come back to you.
**Presenter.** A long-standing member.
**CEU.** givers gain philosophy

### 2. Building relationships
**One thing.** The relationship is the asset. The referral is just what it produces.
**Gaps.** Ref, Ret
**Business hook.** In any long business life, the relationships outlast the companies, the roles and the industries.
**BNI link.** We measure referrals because they are countable. What we are actually building takes longer and is worth more.
**Visitor beat.** Some people in this room have known each other a decade. That is what you are looking at.
**Ask.** Contact one member you have not spoken to outside a meeting.
**Presenter.** Two members with a long partnership.
**CEU.** building relationships trust

### 3. Lifelong learning
**One thing.** The best networker in the room is still learning how to do it.
**Gaps.** CEU KPI
**Business hook.** Every trade requires ongoing training. Selling and referring are skills too, and almost nobody trains them.
**BNI link.** CEUs, BNI Academy, the podcast, this three minutes every week. It is a curriculum, not a formality.
**Visitor beat.** This slot happens every week. It is free, and today it is yours.
**Ask.** Log one CEU this week.
**Presenter.** Education Coordinator.
**CEU.** lifelong learning education

### 4. Positive attitude
**One thing.** The room's mood is made by the people in it, including you.
**Gaps.** Ret, Abs
**Business hook.** Nobody refers work to someone who complains. It is not about optimism, it is about being safe to recommend.
**BNI link.** At 6:45 on a cold Tuesday, attitude is a decision. It shows in the referrals.
**Visitor beat.** If this room feels warm this morning, that is thirty people choosing it, not luck.
**Ask.** Find one member and tell them something good about their business.
**Presenter.** A naturally warm member.
**CEU.** positive attitude

### 5. Accountability
**One thing.** Do what you said you would do, at the time you said.
**Gaps.** Ref, Abs
**Business hook.** Reliability is rarer than talent and worth more. Most businesses lose clients to unreturned calls, not bad work.
**BNI link.** When you take a referral you are holding someone else's reputation. Handle it the way you would want yours handled.
**Visitor beat.** If a member here refers you, they are vouching for you. That is why it works.
**Ask.** Close out every referral and follow-up you are sitting on. Today.
**Presenter.** Vice President.
**CEU.** accountability referral responsibility

### 6. Recognition
**One thing.** Say the name out loud.
**Gaps.** Ret
See `topics-engagement.md` #13 for the fuller treatment. Use this framing when running
it as a core-values week rather than a retention week.
**CEU.** recognition

### 7. Traditions and innovation
**One thing.** The agenda is fixed for a reason; everything around it is yours to improve.
**Gaps.** Ret
**Business hook.** Every business has processes worth defending and habits worth killing, and telling them apart is most of management.
**BNI link.** The structure is what makes BNI work across forty years and eighty countries. How this chapter welcomes people, celebrates and looks after new members is entirely ours.
**Visitor beat.** What you saw today runs the same way in every BNI chapter in the world.
**Ask.** Bring one idea to improve the chapter to the next Success Meeting.
**Presenter.** President.
**CEU.** traditions innovation

## Seasonal

### 8. What do you want from this year?
**Season.** Late January – February.
**One thing.** Decide what BNI is for, this year, in a number.
**Business hook.** January goals fail because they are wishes. "More business" is not a plan.
**BNI link.** Name a number for referrals given, one-to-ones or closed business, and tell someone. The room can help with a target it can see.
**Visitor beat.** Good time of year to be looking at this. That is not an accident.
**Ask.** Write one number down and tell one member.
**Presenter.** President.
**CEU.** goals planning

### 9. Before the year ends
**Season.** May – June (EOFY).
**One thing.** Finish the financial year properly: record it, close it, ask for it.
**Business hook.** Everyone is doing this anyway in June. Add fifteen minutes.
**BNI link.** TYFCB logged, referrals closed out, and the honest question of what the room returned this year.
**Visitor beat.** Members can tell you what this returned them this financial year. Ask one on the way out.
**Ask.** Log your outstanding TYFCB before 30 June.
**Presenter.** Secretary/Treasurer.
**CEU.** TYFCB closed business

### 10. New financial year, new start
**Season.** July.
**One thing.** A clean slate is worth using rather than noting.
**Business hook.** July is when budgets reset and people are briefly willing to change how they work.
**BNI link.** Best month of the second half to invite, to restart one-to-ones and to fix a habit that slipped.
**Visitor beat.** New year, new suppliers. A lot of businesses are looking right now.
**Ask.** Pick the habit that slipped last year and restart it this week.
**Presenter.** Education Coordinator.
**CEU.** goals restart power of one

### 11. The middle of winter
**Season.** August – September.
**One thing.** This is the hard part of the year and it is meant to feel like this.
**Business hook.** Every business has a flat stretch. Recognising it as normal stops people making bad decisions in it.
**BNI link.** Attendance dips, energy dips, and the members who keep showing up through it are the ones with a full diary in November.
**Visitor beat.** If it took effort to get here on a cold morning, you already understand the main requirement.
**Ask.** Turn up next week. That is the whole ask.
**Presenter.** A long-standing member.
**CEU.** consistency motivation

### 12. Book it before Christmas
**Season.** October – November.
**One thing.** There are about eight working weeks left and everyone wants work done before the break.
**Business hook.** Q4 urgency is real in Australia — the shutdown is a hard deadline for trades, and a budget deadline for everyone else.
**BNI link.** Ask for the specific job that has to be finished this year. It is the easiest referral to give all year.
**Visitor beat.** If you need something done before Christmas, this is a room full of people who can do it.
**Ask.** Make your ask about something that must happen before the break.
**Presenter.** A trade member.
**CEU.** urgency specific ask referral

### 13. Thank you, and see you in January
**Season.** December.
**One thing.** Say thank you properly, and mean it.
**Business hook.** The end of the year is the one moment everyone is willing to be sincere.
**BNI link.** Name the referrers, the visitor-bringers, the people who did the unglamorous jobs. Nothing corrective. Nothing about next year's targets.
**Visitor beat.** If you have come along today, you have seen us at our best and least businesslike. Come back in February.
**Ask.** Thank the member who has helped you most this year, to their face.
**Presenter.** President.
**CEU.** gratitude recognition celebration

### 14. Handing over
**Season.** End of a leadership term (usually March and September).
**One thing.** What we learned, and what the next team inherits.
**Business hook.** Handovers are where most organisational knowledge is lost.
**BNI link.** Report honestly against the goals set at the start of the term — what moved, what did not, and why. Then hand the incoming coordinator something better than a blank page.
**Visitor beat.** Every role here rotates. Nobody owns this chapter.
**Ask.** If you have not held a role, put your hand up for the next term.
**Presenter.** Outgoing Education Coordinator.
**CEU.** leadership team roles

### 15. Welcome to the new members
**Season.** Whenever two or more have joined recently.
**One thing.** Here is what the first three months look like, for them and for us.
**Business hook.** New starters everywhere do better when the team, not just the manager, takes responsibility for them.
**BNI link.** Passport to Success, mentors, and the fact that the room's job is to get to them before they get to us.
**Visitor beat.** This is what happens if you join — this week, not eventually.
**Ask.** Book a one-to-one with a member who joined in the last three months.
**Presenter.** Mentor Coordinator.
**CEU.** new member passport mentor
