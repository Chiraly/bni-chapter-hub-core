# Topic bank — attendance, one-to-ones, retention, CEUs

For chapters with high `Abs`, low `Ret`, few one-to-ones, or a red CEU KPI.

---

### 1. Turning up is the whole strategy
**One thing.** You cannot be referred business from a room you are not in.
**Gaps.** Abs
**Business hook.** Miss a client meeting once and it is fine. Miss three and you are no longer in the running, and nobody tells you why.
**BNI link.** Your seat is not just yours — it is your category. When it is empty the room cannot refer that trade at all that week.
**Visitor beat.** Notice how few empty chairs there are. That consistency is the product.
**Ask.** Put every meeting for the next quarter in your calendar now, as an appointment.
**Presenter.** Vice President.
**CEU.** attendance commitment

### 2. Arriving on time, staying to the end
**One thing.** Arriving late and leaving early costs you the two parts that matter most.
**Gaps.** Abs
**Business hook.** If someone you were meeting turned up late without a word, then left before you finished, you would draw a conclusion. Everyone does.
**BNI link.** Open Networking at the start and the conversations at the end are where one-to-ones get booked. The middle is the formal bit. People who arrive for the middle wonder why BNI is not working.
**Visitor beat.** The best fifteen minutes today are the ones before we started. Stay for the ones at the end too.
**Ask.** Next week, arrive fifteen minutes early. Once. See what happens.
**Presenter.** President.
**CEU.** attendance on time meeting

### 3. The substitute program
**One thing.** A substitute is not a formality — it is your business still showing up.
**Gaps.** Abs
**Business hook.** Businesses arrange cover for holidays without a second thought. Nobody calls that weakness.
**BNI link.** A substitute keeps your category live, keeps your referrals flowing, and gives someone else a look at the room. Absence with a substitute is not absence.
**Visitor beat.** If you are here as someone's substitute, you are a guest and you are welcome.
**Ask.** Name your substitute this week and tell them.
**Presenter.** Membership Committee.
**CEU.** substitute attendance

### 4. One coffee a week
**One thing.** One-to-ones are the highest-return hour in BNI, and the easiest to skip.
**Gaps.** Ref, Ret
**Business hook.** Business happens between people who know each other. That is not a networking cliché, it is just how buying works.
**BNI link.** The members getting real value from BNI are, almost without exception, the ones doing one-to-ones consistently. The correlation is not subtle.
**Visitor beat.** Ask any member here how they got their best referral. Most will describe a coffee.
**Ask.** Book one before you leave the room today.
**Presenter.** A member with a strong one-to-one habit.
**CEU.** one to one meeting

### 5. Who you have never sat down with
**One thing.** There is someone in this room you have never had a proper conversation with.
**Gaps.** Ref, Ret
**Business hook.** Every team has people who have worked alongside each other for years and know nothing about each other's work.
**BNI link.** We drift to the same three or four people. The referrals we are missing are with the members we nod at.
**Visitor beat.** If you join, the first month is mostly this — and it is the best part.
**Ask.** Pick the member you know least. Ask them for fifteen minutes.
**Presenter.** Mentor Coordinator.
**CEU.** one to one relationships

### 6. Is BNI working for you?
**One thing.** If it is not working, that is a question with an answer, not a verdict.
**Gaps.** Ret
**Business hook.** Any investment gets reviewed. Nobody keeps paying for something without occasionally asking what it returned.
**BNI link.** Ask it honestly, quarterly. Are you getting referrals? Are your presentations better? Do you know more businesses? Have you made real connections? If the answer is no, the fix is usually activity, not exit.
**Visitor beat.** You are allowed to ask this out loud before you join, and members will answer you straight.
**Ask.** Score yourself out of ten. If it is under six, talk to someone on the Membership Committee this week.
**Presenter.** Vice President or a long-standing member.
**CEU.** getting value BNI working

### 7. The Power of One
**One thing.** Five habits, done weekly, and green looks after itself.
**Gaps.** all
**Business hook.** Every business has a small number of leading indicators. Track those and the lagging ones follow.
**BNI link.** Attendance, referrals given, one-to-ones, visitors, CEUs. Twenty points each, seventy for green. It is not a scoreboard for its own sake — it is the shortest description of what actually works.
**Visitor beat.** This is what membership looks like in practice, before anyone mentions fees.
**Ask.** Pick your weakest of the five and do one thing about it this week.
**Presenter.** Go For Green Coordinator.
**CEU.** power of one traffic lights

### 8. CEUs are free and you are probably already doing them
**One thing.** An hour of listening a week is a full CEU, and most members are already listening to something.
**Gaps.** CEU KPI
**Business hook.** Professional development in most industries is expensive and boring. This is neither.
**BNI link.** The BNI Podcast is about fifteen minutes an episode, free, no account needed. Four episodes is an hour, and an hour is one CEU. Log it in BNI Connect.
**Visitor beat.** All of this is free and public. Go and listen this week whatever you decide about us.
**Ask.** Listen to this week's four episodes on the dashboard and log the CEU.
**Presenter.** Education Coordinator.
**CEU.** chapter education units learning

### 9. The first twelve weeks
**One thing.** What a new member does in their first twelve weeks decides whether they renew.
**Gaps.** Ret
**Business hook.** Onboarding is the cheapest retention work any business can do, and the most commonly skipped.
**BNI link.** The Passport to Success exists for exactly this. New members who complete it meet a dozen members properly; new members who do not spend a year on the edge of the room and then leave.
**Visitor beat.** If you join, you will not be left to work it out alone.
**Ask.** If you have a passport, book your next mentor meeting. If you don't, offer to be a mentor.
**Presenter.** Mentor Coordinator.
**CEU.** passport to success new member mentor

### 10. Why members leave
**One thing.** People do not leave because BNI failed. They leave because they never started.
**Gaps.** Ret
**Business hook.** Gym memberships, subscriptions, courses — the pattern is identical. Non-use precedes cancellation by months.
**BNI link.** Almost every member who does not renew stopped doing one-to-ones long before they stopped paying. The warning sign is visible in the reports months ahead.
**Visitor beat.** Ask a member what they had to do to make this work. They will not say "turn up".
**Ask.** If you have not had a one-to-one in a month, book one today.
**Presenter.** Vice President.
**CEU.** retention renewal member value

### 11. Ask for help before you need it
**One thing.** The room will help you, but only if it knows.
**Gaps.** Ret
**Business hook.** Most people running a small business have a fortnight where things are genuinely hard, and tell nobody.
**BNI link.** This is thirty business owners who have all had that fortnight. Using them is not weakness — it is the entire point of the structure.
**Visitor beat.** This is the part people do not expect and end up valuing most.
**Ask.** Ask the room for one thing that is not a referral.
**Presenter.** A well-liked long-standing member.
**CEU.** support relationships givers gain

### 12. Your BNI Connect profile
**One thing.** An empty profile makes you unreferrable to everyone outside this room.
**Gaps.** Ref, CEU KPI
**Business hook.** Nobody would leave their website half-built and then wonder about enquiries.
**BNI link.** Members in other chapters search BNI Connect to find a trade. If your profile is blank you are invisible to a national network you are already paying for. It is also where CEUs get logged.
**Visitor beat.** Membership is not just this room — it is every chapter in the country.
**Ask.** Fill in your profile properly this week. Twenty minutes.
**Presenter.** A member who uses BNI Connect well.
**CEU.** BNI Connect profile

### 13. Recognition is not a formality
**One thing.** Naming what people did makes them do it again.
**Gaps.** Ret, all
**Business hook.** Recognition is the cheapest management tool available and the most consistently underused.
**BNI link.** Notable Networkers, green members, the person who quietly brought three visitors. Saying it out loud, by name, changes behaviour more than any policy reminder.
**Visitor beat.** Watch who gets thanked this morning. It tells you what this room actually values.
**Ask.** Thank one member properly this week, in person, for something specific.
**Presenter.** Vice President.
**CEU.** recognition celebrate

### 14. The meeting is a rehearsal
**One thing.** Everything you practise here, you use out there.
**Gaps.** Ret, Abs
**Business hook.** Most business owners never get to practise describing what they do. They just do it badly at the moment it counts.
**BNI link.** Fifty weeks of thirty-second presentations is fifty rehearsals. Members who take it seriously get noticeably better at selling, everywhere.
**Visitor beat.** Whatever you decide, watch how people describe their business today, and take the good bits.
**Ask.** Time your weekly presentation before you deliver it next week.
**Presenter.** Education Coordinator.
**CEU.** weekly presentation practice
