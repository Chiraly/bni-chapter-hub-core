BNI's own tools

Worth knowing about

  • myBNI The front door to BNI Connect, with the same username and password. Log your CEUs here, record referrals and one-to-ones, and look up members in other chapters.
  • Global Brand Hub BNI's official logos, brand guidelines, PowerPoint and Canva templates, and the Visitor Week toolkits. Start here before you make anything with BNI branding on it.
  • BNI Global Academy BNI's online training, in your own time. Courses count towards your CEUs — log them in BNI Connect like anything else.
  • Ask I.V.A.N. BNI's chatbot, straight in your browser. Ask it a question about how something in BNI works rather than hunting for the answer.
